How about Europe VAT TAX?
About Europe VAT TAX
We offer two shipping options for EU customers

  1. Express shipping-DHL/FEDEX
    1. Shipping fee does not include vat tax
    2. Custom or local delivery company may randomly investigate package value.
      We can't declare low value for your package

  2. Commercial shipping
    1. Shipping fee includes vat tax
    2. Definitely don't have to pay vat tax when packages arrive in the destination country.


Delivery Issues and Package Destruction Policy
We strive to ensure that all orders are delivered successfully. However, in cases where a package cannot be delivered due to reasons such as an incorrect address or the recipient not being available, we offer one free redispatch attempt.
Upon a failed delivery attempt, we will notify you of the issue and provide instructions on how to proceed. You must respond to this notification within the specified timeframe to arrange for redispatch or provide updated delivery information.
If you fail to respond within the required timeframe, the package will be considered abandoned and we will not be held responsible for the destruction of the package.
Please ensure that your shipping information is accurate and that you are available to receive your package or respond to delivery notifications promptly.
